Carve-o-Lantern

Carve your perfect jack-o'-lantern in VR, light it up, and snap a spooky photo.

Grab a knife and carve the pumpkin of your dreams — no mess, no slime. Pick your pumpkin, choose your tools, and sculpt faces, patterns and stamps right in front of you. Rotate it, undo mistakes, then drop in a candle and watch your creation glow.

Finish in photo mode against haunted forests, moonlit pumpkin patches and witchy villages, and save the shot to share. A cozy, spooky little Halloween ritual, any time of year.

Details

  • Platform: Meta Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest 3S, Quest Pro
  • Single player · Seated or standing · Touch controllers
  • Works offline · No account, ads or tracking
