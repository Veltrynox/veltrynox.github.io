Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Carve-o-Lantern (“the app”), developed by Assart (“we”, “us”), handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash or usage tracking, and it does not send any data to us or to third parties. The app works fully offline.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location, and we do not receive any body, hand, eye or face tracking data.

2. Photos you take in the app

When you use photo mode, the image of your carved pumpkin is saved only on your headset, in the Pictures/PumpkinCarve folder. We never receive these images. If you choose to share a photo, the app opens your headset’s built-in share menu; anything you share is sent by you, through the service you pick, and is governed by that service’s own policy.

3. How we use information

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use it for any purpose, and we do not sell, rent or share it.

4. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Photos saved by the app stay on your device until you delete them, which you can do at any time from your headset’s file or gallery app. Uninstalling the app removes its settings from your device.

5. Meta platform

The app is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta may collect information about your purchase and use of apps on its platform as described in the Meta Privacy Policy. That collection is controlled by Meta, not by us.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, contact us at ivan.larinin@icloud.com.
